Weekly theme — FOMC digested, RQI thesis holds, ERH confirmed

The week that looked like a loss on Wednesday turned into a net gain by Friday. The FOMC voted 9-3 to hold at 3.50–3.75% on Wednesday 07/29, but the market's initial hawkish read — rates higher for longer, three dissenting votes, Chair Warsh deliberately withholding forward guidance — produced a single-session −$8.00 reaction that was the largest loss in the 13-session tracking window. What followed was more telling: Thursday's +$8.43 and Friday's +$9.90 fully reversed the FOMC reaction and added $10.33 net, suggesting the market's initial read was an overreaction to the hawkish framing rather than a fundamental repricing of the rate outlook.

The week's analytical milestone was not the FOMC at all — it was ERH. After three one-session Green spikes that each reversed immediately (07/22, 07/28, and again on 07/29), ERH held Green quadrant for two consecutive sessions on Thursday and Friday, satisfying the framework's two-session confirmation requirement for the first time in the tracking window. Z1Y +0.33 and D/P −7.32% — inside its own −7.74% average — confirmed on both sessions simultaneously. The ROC-primary distribution flag remains unchanged; a Green quadrant cross is a valuation signal, not a distribution quality reclassification. ERH also declared its tenth consecutive monthly distribution increase on Friday: $0.08784/share, ex-div 08/13/2026, trailing 12-month payout $1.0211 (+7.3% cumulative since December 2025).

RQI compressed for the tenth session of the clean window (one FOMC artifact reversal on 07/29, nine clean sessions). Z1Y −1.28 at Friday's close — a +1.37pt cumulative recovery from the −2.65 peak dislocation on 07/17. The rights offering overhang that contaminated the z-score through 07/15 has been fully absorbed. BST remains in Green (Z1Y +0.32) with NAV stable at $48.57 following the prior week's six-session decline. The $5K add decision is QTI's to execute — RNP (deepest REIT dislocation at −5.52pt from its own norm, 57% NII), RQI (thesis active), and BST (Green, stable NAV) are the surface candidates.
